About TravelMate – Smart Tourist Day-Visit Planner

Prepared by Rukshana Roshan (Registration No: E2410132) for the Department of Information Technology, Faculty of Information Technology, University of Moratuwa (Academic Year 2026).

Destination Places Gallery
1.1 Problem Statement

Tourists visiting Atulugama, Bandaragama, Kalutara, and surrounding Western Province regions face fragmented travel information scattered across social media and multiple websites. Visitors often struggle to gauge approximate travel times and sequence multiple destinations into a feasible single-day itinerary.

1.2 Project Aim

TravelMate delivers a centralized web platform that helps both local and foreign tourists explore verified attractions within 25 km of Atulugama and generate structured, time-optimized day itineraries tailored to individual interests and time limits.

In-Scope System Capabilities
  • 10 verified tourist destinations within ~25 km of Atulugama.
  • Categorization (Recreation, Adventure, Nature, Beach, Cultural, Historical, Waterfall).
  • One-day itinerary builder with nearest-neighbor route optimization.
  • Leaflet & Google Maps directional integration.
  • Administrator CRUD management portal for attraction records.
Explicit Project Limitations
  • Hotel booking and room reservations are outside the scope.
  • Commercial online payment processing is excluded.
  • Guaranteed real-time public transit scheduling is not supported.
Frontend Tier HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Bootstrap 5.3, Bootstrap Icons, Leaflet Maps. Fully responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
Backend Tier Java 17+, Spring Boot 3.3 REST API (Spring Web, Spring Data JPA, Spring Validation) with Route Optimizer Service.
Database Tier MySQL 8.0 (Production) & In-Memory H2 (Zero-Config Development Profile) supporting TOURIST_ATTRACTION table schema.
Safety & Non-Functional Requirements (NFR-006 to NFR-009, BR-007)
  • Distances and visiting durations are mathematical estimates and do not represent guaranteed real-time traffic speeds.
  • The system does not guarantee immediate road condition availability or entrance pass reservations.
  • Visitors should exercise personal caution at natural waterfall streams (Thudugala Ella) and follow temple etiquette at Buddhist sites.
